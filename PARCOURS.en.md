# Reading path

The lattice can be explored in every direction. For a first reading, here is a thread of sixteen steps, from the book's question to its conclusion. Each step points to an element; from there, "Path to" shows what it rests on, "Consequences" what follows from it.

## I. The question

**1.** T001. How "can" some men make others work for their desire? This power is an effect of structure (Marx), but structures act through the affects (Spinoza).

**2.** T002. Contemporary capitalism produces "happy dominated" people. Domination must be rethought without resorting to "voluntary servitude".

## II. Making others do

**3.** D01, T003. The conatus is the energy of desire. Desiring to do is legitimate; having others do, enlisting other powers (D02) in the service of a master-desire (D03), raises a political question.

**4.** T006, T007. In an economy with a division of labour, life goes through money: the wage relation is first dependence, hence domination, the most powerful there is.

**5.** T008, T009. Desire is heteronomous: its objects come to it from outside. Voluntary servitude does not exist; there is only passional servitude, and it is universal.

**6.** T014. Mobilising means aligning: the model of the angle α (see the [figure of the angle α](#alpha)).

**7.** T015, T019. Regimes of mobilisation change: hunger, then consumption, then the neoliberal firm, which wants α = 0 and pushes domination as far as tyranny.

## III. Joyful self-movers

**8.** T021. The neoliberal project: making work itself desired (see the view of the [regimes of affects](#regimes)).

**9.** T022, T025. Consent, alienation and interiority do not exist as they are usually understood; constraint and consent are only sad or joyful affects.

**10.** T024, T027, T028. Where joyful obedience comes from: power reigns better through love; love of the master; vocational images.

**11.** T029, T034. The firm aims at possessing souls, but the background of sad affects remains.

## IV. Domination, emancipation

**12.** T035, T036. Even joyful, domination reserves the desirable for some and keeps it from others.

**13.** T038. Exploitation is passional: a capture of power of acting, not an extraction of surplus value.

**14.** T040. The récommune: the enterprise constituted as a radical democracy.

**15.** T042, T043. Change without free will: indignation makes one "become perpendicular".

**16.** T048, T049. True communism is a horizon; but not all passional regimes are equal, and progress is the enrichment of life in joyful affects.
